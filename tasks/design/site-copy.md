# UI Design Review

## Pre-Build Review
The hero introduces Nate; the timeline tells his story; fundraising explains the purpose; merchandise offers another way to help; coverage shows the community response. Keep the campaign typography, shapes, and family voice.

## Risks To Address Before Coding
Shorter prose must preserve useful instructions, meaningful link names, factual details, and distinct payment statuses. Avoid generic slogans and repeated reassurance.

## Post-Build Screenshot Review
Reviewed the rendered desktop homepage at 1280×720 and mobile pages at 390×844. The hero's “Donate to CHOP” fits on one line. The shorter fundraising and merchandise sections keep their hierarchy and breathing room. The donation form presents one feedback message beneath the amount. Birthday closure, missing-page actions, and the donation-return missing-details message remain readable with usable buttons.

## Remaining Polish Or UX Issues
No copy-related layout issues found. Existing third-party media availability and local Stripe configuration were not changed. Automated reduced-motion and no-JavaScript checks pass.
